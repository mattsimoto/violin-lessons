Play together

  1. Tune first: violin G3–D4–A4–E5; ukulele high G4–C4–E4–A4. Accordion uses the 41-key / 120-bass layout.
  2. Let the featured instrument play the melody. Keep the backing parts softer.
  3. Learn each part slowly, then listen to the full trio. Play starts with one bar of count-in.
One beat = one quarter note.
